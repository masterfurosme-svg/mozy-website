Overview

A remodel that respects your time and your home.

Kitchen remodeling in Florida generally runs $15,000 to $60,000, depending on cabinetry, countertops, layout changes, and appliances. As a licensed general contractor, Mozy Construction gives you a fixed written estimate up front and manages demolition, plumbing, electrical, cabinetry, countertops, and finishing under one contract — so you never coordinate separate trades yourself.

What's included

  • Design consultation and a clear, itemized written estimate
  • Demolition and haul-away
  • Custom cabinetry and countertop installation
  • Plumbing and electrical updates (permitted)
  • Tile, backsplash, and flooring
  • Final finishing, clean-up, and walkthrough

Common questions

How long does a kitchen remodel take?
Most kitchen remodels take 3 to 6 weeks, depending on scope and material lead times. Because we manage the whole project under one contract, demolition, plumbing, cabinetry, and finishing are coordinated internally rather than left to you.

Is Mozy Construction licensed for kitchen remodeling?
Yes. Mozy Construction is a licensed and insured Florida general contractor, A Construction license (CGC 1535189), serving Orlando, Broward County, and Miami.
