Common questions

Which flooring holds up best in Florida?
Tile and luxury vinyl handle Florida humidity best, while engineered hardwood is a good mid-range option. Mozy Construction helps you choose the right material for each room and gives you a written estimate.

Is Mozy Construction licensed?
